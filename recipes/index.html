Shakshuka

Shakshuka With Feta (modified from NYT recipe) YIELD 4 to 6 servings TIME 50 minutes

Ingredients

3 tablespoons olive oil 1 large onion, chopped 1 large red bell pepper, chopped 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced 2 teaspoons ground cumin 2 teaspoons smoked paprika 2 teaspoons chili powder (optional) 1/4 teaspoon cayenne, or to taste 1 (28-ounce) can whole plum or diced tomatoes 5 ounces feta cheese, crumbled (about 1 1/4 cups) 6 large eggs

Instructions

  1. Drain the tomatoes. They don’t need to be completely drained but try to remove the majority of the water, by pouring it out.
  2. Optional: Put the eggs in some lukewarm water so they cook faster later.
  3. Heat oil in a cast iron (or large skillet) over medium heat.
  4. Add onion and bell pepper. Cook gently until very soft, about 20 minutes.
  5. Add garlic and cook until tender, 1 to 2 minutes;
  6. Stir in cumin, paprika, chili powder, and cayenne, and cook 1 minute.
  7. Pour in tomatoes
  8. Sprinkle in crumbled feta.
  9. Gently crack eggs into skillet over tomatoes. (Note: It’s easier to put the eggs in if you create some divets for the eggs about 1/3 or 1/2 way from the center of the skillet)
  10. Put a lid over the eggs until eggs are just set, 7 to 10 minutes.
  11. Take off lid for about 2 minutes to thicken

Tom Kha (inspired soup)

Modified from https://40aprons.com/tom-kha-soup-whole30/

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • half of one onion thinly sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic chopped
  • 16 Thai chilies cut small
  • 3.25-inch slices galangal or ginger
  • 1 lemongrass stalk pounded with the side of a knife and cut into 2-inch long pieces ** haven’t tried this
  • 1 tablespoon lemongrass stalk
  • 2 teaspoons red Thai curry paste
  • 4 cups chicken broth see Notes if vegan or on Whole30
  • 4 cups canned full-fat coconut cream unsweetened, or full-fat unsweetened coconut milk
  • 2 medium chicken breasts cut into bite-sized pieces or 1 16oz block tofu
  • 16 ounces white mushroom caps sliced
  • 1-2 tablespoons coconut sugar see Notes for Whole30 option
  • 1.5 - 2 tablespoons fish sauce plus more to taste, see Notes if on Whole30 or vegan
  • 2-3 tablespoons fresh lime juice
  • 2-3 green onions sliced thin
  • fresh cilantro chopped, for garnish

Instructions

  1. In a medium pot, heat the coconut oil (1 Tbsp) over medium heat. Add the onion (1/2 onion thinly sliced), garlic (2 cloves), jalapeno or thai chile (<=16), galangal or ginger (3 1/4 in slices), lemongrass (1 stalk pounded), and red curry paste (2 tsp) and cook, stirring frequently, for 5 minutes, or until onions are softened.
  2. Add chicken broth (4 cups) and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 30 minutes.
  3. Strain out the aromatics (the garlic, onions, lemongrass, and ginger) and discard.
  4. Add in coconut cream or milk (4 cups), chicken breast (or tofu or shrimp), and mushrooms.
  5. Simmer until chicken breast pieces are just cooked through, then add fish sauce (1.5-2 Tbsp), coconut aminos (or coconut sugar) (1-2 Tbsp), and lime juice (2-3 Tbsp), plus more of each to taste.
  6. Cook 2 minutes, then ladle into serving bowls and top with sliced green onions (2-3) and fresh cilantro.

Bock Choy and Mushroom Stir Fry

Modified from https://omnivorescookbook.com/bok-choy-and-mushroom-stir-fry/

Ingredients

Sauce

  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon vegetarian oyster sauce (or regular oyster sauce)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil

Slurry

  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons water

Stir-Fry

  • 1 lb (450 g) baby bok choy , cut to large bite-size pieces
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons peanut oil
  • 1 lb (450 g) brown mushrooms , halved (or quartered if the size is too big)
  • 4 dried Chinese chili peppers
  • 2 garlic cloves , minced
  • 1 thumb ginger , minced
  • 2 green onions , sliced

Instructions

  1. Mix the sauce ingredients in a small bowl and set aside.
  2. Add the slurry ingredients in a small bowl and stir to mix well.
  3. Heat 1/4 water in a large skillet over medium high heat until hot. When the water starts to boil, add the baby bok choy and sprinkle with a pinch of salt. Cover the pan and let steam for 30 seconds. Uncover the pan and stir the bok choy. Cover again and cook for another 30 seconds, until the baby bok choy has softened. Transfer the bok choy into a big platter.
  4. Wipe the pan clean with paper towels and add 2 tablespoons of the oil, turn to high heat. Add the mushrooms. Let sear for 1 minute without touching. Then flip the mushrooms, cook and stir occasionally, for 2 to 3 minutes, until the mushrooms are browned and there’s no juice left in the pan.
  5. Move the mushrooms to one side of the pan. Pour the remaining 1/2 tablespoon oil on the other side and add the chili peppers, garlic, ginger and green onion. Stir a few times until the chili pepper turns dark red. Then stir everything together.
  6. Pour in the sauce. Stir and cook for 30 seconds. Stir the slurry mixture again until the cornstarch is fully dissolved. Swirl into the pan. Cook and stir until the sauce thickens, then transfer everything to a big plate. Serve hot as a side or over steamed rice as a main dish.
